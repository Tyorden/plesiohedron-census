%C (COMMENTS — each paragraph pasted separately, signed with ~~~~ so the OEIS editor auto-signs; do not type a name or date by hand)
The Laves graph plesiohedron is the 17-faced space-filling polyhedron (4^6 5^6 6^2 8^3 faces, f-vector (30, 45, 17)) that is the Voronoi cell of the vertex set of the Laves graph (the srs net), i.e. of Wyckoff position 8a of space group I4_132 (IT 214), generating point (1/8, 1/8, 1/8), site symmetry .32; its honeycomb has 4 cell types (translation classes), each cell with 17 face-neighbors. Polyforms are face-connected clusters of cells, counted up to lattice translation only.
The cell is the well-known 17-faced plesiohedron of the Laves graph (Coxeter 1955; Schoen 2008); it is not new. Only the polyform sequences are new here.
The honeycomb is chiral (all honeycomb symmetries are proper), so the one-sided and free counts coincide.
The tiling, its symmetry group (order 24 modulo lattice translations, 24 proper) and the counts are certified in exact rational arithmetic: facet-pairing tiling certificate verified by two independent implementations; the Burnside identity |G|*free(n) = Sum_g Fix_g(n) checked exactly for n <= 6; all terms n <= 6 reproduced by a second, independently written enumerator.
Computed with the assistance of Claude (Anthropic); every count produced by the same published enumerator that generated A398957-A398959, with the exact-arithmetic verification above. - ~~~~
