a-file for the Laves graph plesiohedron polyform sequences (fixed / one-sided / free).

The Laves graph plesiohedron is the 17-faced space-filling polyhedron (4^6 5^6 6^2 8^3 faces, f-vector (30, 45, 17)) that is the Voronoi cell of the vertex set of the Laves graph (the srs net), i.e. of Wyckoff position 8a of space group I4_132 (IT 214), generating point (1/8, 1/8, 1/8), site symmetry .32; its honeycomb has 4 cell types (translation classes), each cell with 17 face-neighbors.

The cell is the well-known 17-faced plesiohedron of the Laves graph (Coxeter 1955; Schoen 2008); it is not new. Only the polyform sequences are new here.

Program: the table-driven Redelmeier enumerator published as the a-file of A398957 (enumerate.cpp).  Save a tables block below to tables.txt and run:  ./enumerate tables.txt N
With TABLES-FULL the output columns are n, fixed, free.
With TABLES-PROPER the 'free' column is the one-sided count (fixed column identical).
Exact generating data (space group, point, lattice basis, vertex coordinates): COORDS.md in the same package.
