the Pn-3m 7-facet cell is a 7-faced space-filling polyhedron (3^2 4^3 6^2 faces) arising as the Voronoi cell of the orbit of the point (1/8, 1/6, 5/12) under space group Pn-3m (IT 224); its honeycomb has 48 cell types (translation classes), each cell with 7 face-neighbors. Polyforms are face-connected clusters of cells, counted up to lattice translation only.
The tiling, its symmetry group (order 48 modulo lattice translations, 24 proper) and the counts are certified in exact rational arithmetic: facet-pairing tiling certificate verified by two independent implementations, and the Burnside identity |G|*free(n) = Sum_g Fix_g(n) checked exactly for n <= 4.
The cell's combinatorial type is not matched against the records checked as of 2026-09-01 (Schmitt 2016 printed cubic tables incl. exact recomputation at every printed representative point, Bernhard 2026 printed cell data, classical space-filler lists); this is a diligence statement, not a proof of novelty.
