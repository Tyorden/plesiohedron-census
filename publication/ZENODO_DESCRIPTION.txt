DESCRIPTION:
Code, certificates, catalog, and per-shape data from MINT, a gated agentic search for
space-filling polyhedra (plesiohedra) in the cubic, tetragonal, trigonal and hexagonal
space groups.

Contents: the complete harness (exact-arithmetic pipeline: orbit expansion under frozen
space-group operations, floating-point Voronoi proposal, exact rational confirmation with a
proven neighbour cutoff, canonical planar codes; an exact Gram-matrix metric for the
non-cubic families); the frozen operation store for all 230 space groups (confirmed
operation by operation against a recovered 2016 derivation, zero mismatches on all 229
comparable groups); the pre-registered gates and every gate result; the type stores of the
three sweeps (1,597 cubic orbits; 148,816 tetragonal and 108,580 hexagonal-family
evaluations); full publication packages for seven certified cubic cells, including exact
vertex coordinates, tiling certificates, symmetry certification, polyform counts with
Burnside verification, and renders; certificate tables for 165 further tetragonal and
hexagonal-family cells with a pre-registered open/wall classification (115 open, 41 wall, 9
one-sided at the stored witness); the digitizations of Schmitt's 2016 printed tables (881
cubic, 1,476 tetragonal and 958 trigonal/hexagonal rows, the last blind re-keyed with zero
discrepancies); and THE CATALOG, version 5: 1,583 combinatorial types (102 cubic-first, 789
tetragonal-first, 692 hexagonal-first; 196 distinct f-vectors; observed maximum 35 facets),
each with its exact generating point, group, axial ratio where relevant, f-vector, facet-size
multiset, automorphism order, canonical code, every sighting, certificate status, open/wall
verdict and type-level status against Schmitt's printed representatives, in JSON and CSV with
SHA-256 sums and an independent recount script.

Two cubic cells are named (Satchelhedron, IT 220, f-vector (16,25,11), a wall point of the
24d line that Schmitt's grid provably never meets; Ordenhedron, IT 201, (20,33,15), an open
presentation with trivial symmetry); the other five cubic cells carry systematic labels
(group, f-vector, canonical type identifier) while two print-only catalogs (Engel 1981, Koch
1972) are read. No cell outside the cubic system is named.

Novelty wording throughout is catalog-relative by rule: every type is "not matched against
the records checked as of 2026-09-04" (classical catalogs, Bernhard 2026, and Schmitt's 2016
printed tables at f-vector and, at every printed representative point, at type level); this
is never a claim of being new to science. The observed literature maximum is 38 facets; the
refereed bound is 92.
