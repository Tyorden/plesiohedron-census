## What "naming" a polyhedron actually is
There is no registry. A name sticks by publication + uptake: a paper (arXiv/
Zenodo at minimum) that defines the object with exact coordinates, proves the
tiling, states the diligence, and uses the name; OEIS sequences whose titles
carry the name; then Wikipedia/MathWorld/community adoption or not. Bernhard's
Josehedron is the exact precedent: self-published + arXiv, named for his
daughter, with hooks (integer coordinates, roundness record, naming joke). His
diligence was: none (checked no catalogs). Ours already exceeds his by a wide
margin for every finalist.
